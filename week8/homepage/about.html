About us

Dough & Co. was founded with a simple dream—to turn an everyday treat into something truly special. Behind the brand is Dheekshith Challa, a passionate entrepreneur who believes that great food has the power to bring people together and create lasting memories. What started as an idea soon became a mission: to build a donut shop where quality, creativity, and customer happiness come first. Rather than offering just another dessert, Dheekshith envisioned a place where every donut is handcrafted with care, made from fresh ingredients, and served with pride. As the founder, Dheekshith Challa is driven by innovation and a commitment to excellence. Every recipe, flavor, and customer experience reflects his vision of creating a brand that people trust, recommend, and return to. His goal isn’t simply to sell donuts—it’s to build a community around moments of joy, celebration, and togetherness. Today, Dough & Co. stands as a reflection of that vision: a modern donut destination where tradition meets creativity, and every customer is welcomed like family. With a focus on freshness, consistency, and unforgettable flavors, the journey is only just beginning. Founded by Dheekshith Challa. Built with passion. Crafted to bring smiles—one donut at a time.
